What will actually break you

Three failures reproduced. Each was confirmed by a second, independent model family before it was filed, and each fix below stayed green across 500 re-forks.

What it refused to tell you

A tool that reports everything it suspects trains you to ignore it. Cassandra files only what it reproduced, and says plainly when it could not.

Why this couldn't exist until now

Rehearsing failure means running hundreds of realistic, disposable copies of a live system. That was always too expensive, so teams tested a handful of guesses and met the rest in production. Token Factory Sandboxes made forking real execution state cost cents, which turns the whole approach over: instead of predicting what might break, Cassandra breaks it, watches, and brings back proof.
